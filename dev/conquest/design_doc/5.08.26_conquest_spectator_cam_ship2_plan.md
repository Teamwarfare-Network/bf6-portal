## Mitigation C — How the Spectator Body Lives in the World

Under Mitigation A (the umbrella plan's first choice), the spectator stays undeployed. The deploy-screen UI was supposed to be invisible because the Fixed camera would override it; instead, I-4 showed the engine deploy-screen camera wins. Mitigation C re-frames the spectator as a **deployed-but-camera-locked** player whose combat HUD persists so they can watch the match unfold.

**Key context (drives much of the simplification below):** the spectator is already deployed when they click COACH. Triple-tap on the Player Ready Panel is gated on `isPlayerDeployed`, and the admin's full ready dialog is invoked from the same deployed-player surfaces. Pre-live boundary enforcement also keeps players inside their HQ trigger pre-match, so the body is at HQ when they click. We do NOT need to re-deploy — we just swap the camera.

1. **Body stays put.** Player is at HQ pre-match (already deployed, already inside own-HQ trigger). No `UndeployPlayer` / `DeployPlayer` round-trip. The body is immobile-via-spectator-control (engine input still active, but they're watching the Fixed camera so movement is moot).
2. **Attach the Godot Fixed camera directly.** Since the player is already deployed, `mod.SetCameraTypeForPlayer(player, Cameras.Fixed, id)` takes effect immediately (this is the "deployed" side of the I-4 finding — that path works).
3. **Combat HUD persists.** Per user direction (2026-05-08): the spectator needs to watch the match. They keep tickets, flag states, capture progress, all the top-HUD + combat-HUD widgets they had before clicking COACH. **No `twlConquestHudDestroyPlayer(pid)` call.**
4. **Skip the OOB classifier for their pid.** Even though the body is parked at HQ, defensively skip boundary classification — guards against any zone-state hiccup (engine event reordering, edge-case zone math, etc.). Adding `if (pid === State.players.spectatorPid) return undefined;` early in `getDesiredBoundaryViolationKind` prevents an OOB warning from flashing on the Fixed camera view.
5. **No exit path.** Same one-way commitment as Mitigation A. Disconnect or match-end frees the slot.

Concrete deltas vs. the umbrella's Mitigation-A spec:

| Step | Mitigation A (umbrella) | Mitigation C (this plan, HUD-persistence variant) |
|---|---|---|
| Click handler step 4 | `mod.UndeployPlayer(eventPlayer)` | **No deploy-state mutation.** Player is already deployed at HQ pre-match. |
| Click handler step 5 | `mod.SetCameraTypeForPlayer(...)` (queued but never visible due to I-4) | `mod.SetCameraTypeForPlayer(...)` directly in click handler — works because player is already deployed (the "deployed" side of I-4 succeeds). |
| Combat HUD destruction | Yes (`twlConquestHudDestroyPlayer(pid)` on claim) | **No — HUD persists** so spectator can watch the match. |
| Boundary OOB | Free via `!isPlayerDeployed` early-return at [`enforcement.ts:225`](../src/boundary/enforcement.ts#L225) | New defensive early-return: `if (pid === State.players.spectatorPid) return undefined;` at [`enforcement.ts:223-227`](../src/boundary/enforcement.ts#L223) |
| Match-end camera reset | `mod.SetCameraTypeForPlayer(spec, Cameras.FirstPerson)` | Same call; the engine restores normal camera. |

Everything else in the umbrella plan's Ship 2 spec carries over unchanged: state, ready-mark, panel close, world-log message (gated on string approval), cross-viewer broadcast, lifecycle cleanup, COACH button on admin's full ready dialog, map-config field for remaining 8 maps, scoreboard skip via `getActivePlayers` filter.

### Camera attachment timing

Since the spectator is already deployed when they click COACH, the camera can be attached **directly in the click handler** — no async hook, no timer, no `DeployPlayer` round-trip. This is the "deployed" side of the I-4 finding and was empirically observed to work in v1.476 (after the user pressed DEPLOY, the camera attached immediately).

**Defensive `OnPlayerDeployed` re-attach** is added regardless: if the spectator's body somehow re-deploys mid-spectate (vehicle-spawn collision, mod-side bug, manual `DeployPlayer` call from another flow, etc.), the hook re-attaches the Fixed camera so the spectator's view stays consistent. Mirrors the `onHqSeatPendingPlayerDeployed` precedent at [`player-deploy.ts:72`](../src/index/player-deploy.ts#L72) — same "if pid matches a pending state, do something special inside the deploy event" pattern. Idempotent + cheap; ~5 LOC.

### Input restriction (v1.479)

**Problem found in v1.478 SP test:** `mod.SetCameraTypeForPlayer` swaps the per-pid view but does NOT touch input handling. The soldier body still responded to WASD / fire / reload / slot-switch even though the player's view was the static Fixed camera. Effectively the spectator could walk their (invisible-to-them) body around HQ.

**Fix:** lock all relevant inputs at claim time via `mod.EnableInputRestriction(player, RestrictedInputs.X, true)`. Restricted set:

| Category | Inputs |
|---|---|
| Movement | `MoveForwardBack`, `MoveLeftRight`, `Sprint`, `Crouch`, `Prone`, `Jump` |
| Combat | `FireWeapon`, `Reload`, `Zoom`, `CycleFire`, `CyclePrimary` |
| Interaction | `Interact` |
| Slot/weapon switch | `SelectCharacterGadget`, `SelectOpenGadget`, `SelectMelee`, `SelectPrimary`, `SelectSecondary`, `SelectThrowable` |

**Not restricted:** `CameraPitch`, `CameraYaw` — Fixed camera ignores look-input by definition; not restricting avoids any post-match input-state restoration ordering risk. Match-end / fresh-setup / disconnect paths all release the lock so a normal first-person view restores cleanly.

### Body-hide via PCT-pattern sealed room (v1.479)

**Problem found in v1.478 SP test:** with input now locked the body stops moving, but it's still visible to teammates standing motionless at HQ. Cosmetic but ugly. The umbrella plan §"Out of Scope" punted on Mitigation B (PCT-style hidden body) as too heavy; v1.478 inherited that. The user pushed back, pointing at the existing PCT reference: [`reference_implementations/reference_nodone_cinematic_camera/main_module.ts`](../reference_implementations/reference_nodone_cinematic_camera/main_module.ts) (`SpawnDirectorControlRoom` at line 2486). v1.479 layers the body-hide aspect of B onto our C foundation.

**Methodology (per AGENTS.md non-copy policy — pattern adapted, code original):**

PCT (`SpawnDirectorControlRoom`):
1. Computes room center as `camPos + (0, yOffset, 0)`. Default `yOffset = -50` (underground); `+300` (sky) used as fallback when underground hits water.
2. Spawns 4 × `FiringRange_Wall_2048_01`, 1 × `FiringRange_Floor_A`, 1 × `FiringRange_Ceiling_02` around the center, with carefully-tuned overlap math (PCT room interior is 5mm — director is forced into a single point, fully sealed).
3. After teleport: `WaterCheck()` runs after `mod.Wait(1)`. If `Player.IsInWater(dirPlayer)` (`mod.GetSoldierStateBool(player, SoldierStateBool.IsInWater)`), unspawns underground room and respawns at sky offset `+300`, then re-teleports.
4. `UnspawnDirectorControlRoom()` cleans up via `mod.UnspawnObject` for each tracked handle.

Our adaptation (simplified for single-fixed-camera use case):

| Aspect | PCT (full) | Ours (v1.479) |
|---|---|---|
| Spatial spawns | 6 (4 walls + floor + ceiling) | 6 (same — keeps the body fully sealed against any sky-fly observer) |
| Spawn position | `camPos + (0, -50, 0)` underground default; sky fallback | `camPos + (0, -50, 0)` underground; same +300 sky fallback on water hit |
| Room interior | 5mm cube (PCT walks the director around in free-cam) | 5mm cube (we don't walk; same dim is fine — body just sits at center) |
| Water check | Yes | Yes — mirror PCT's pattern via `Timers.setTimeout(1000)` + `mod.GetSoldierStateBool(player, SoldierStateBool.IsInWater)` |
| Cleanup | `UnspawnDirectorControlRoom()` | Mirror — iterate tracked handles, `mod.UnspawnObject` each, clear state |
| Cleanup hooks | director-leaves / mode-changes | match-end / disconnect / fresh-setup |

**State (added to spectator-action.ts module-local, NOT to State.players since it's per-mode-not-per-pid):**

```ts
let _spectatorHideRoomObjects: mod.SpatialObject[] = [];
let _spectatorHideRoomCenter: { x: number; y: number; z: number } | null = null;
```

**Functions (new in spectator-action.ts):**

- `spawnSpectatorHideRoom(skySpawn: boolean): boolean` — resolves camera position via `mod.GetObjectPosition(mod.GetFixedCamera(camId))`, computes room center, spawns 6 spatial objects, tracks handles. Idempotent (no-op if already spawned). Returns true on success.
- `despawnSpectatorHideRoom(): void` — iterates handles, calls `mod.UnspawnObject` for each, clears tracking state.
- `teleportSpectatorIntoHideRoom(player: mod.Player): void` — `mod.Teleport(player, roomCenterVector, 0)`. No-op if room not spawned.
- `tryWaterCheckAndRebuildRoom(player: mod.Player, pid: number): void` — `Timers.setTimeout(1000)` → if `mod.GetSoldierStateBool(player, SoldierStateBool.IsInWater)`, `despawnSpectatorHideRoom()` + `spawnSpectatorHideRoom(true)` + `teleportSpectatorIntoHideRoom(player)`.

**Wiring in `enterSpectatorMode`** (after `attachSpectatorCameraIfDeployed` + `applySpectatorInputLock`):

```ts
if (spawnSpectatorHideRoom(false /* underground default */)) {
    teleportSpectatorIntoHideRoom(eventPlayer);
    tryWaterCheckAndRebuildRoom(eventPlayer, pid);
}
```

**Cleanup wiring** (added to all three lifecycle hooks):

- `onSpectatorPlayerLeave(pid)`: `despawnSpectatorHideRoom()` if pid was the spectator
- `onSpectatorMatchEnd()`: `despawnSpectatorHideRoom()` after camera reset
- `onSpectatorFreshSetup()`: `despawnSpectatorHideRoom()` (defensive — match-end path is the normal cleanup)

**Not pursued (out of v1.479 scope):**

- Per-map authored room location. PCT computes room from the camera position; our maps each have one spec cam; per-map computation is automatic.
- Walls in non-FiringRange dimensions (`FiringRange_Wall_1024_01`, etc.). 2048 is sufficient for our 5mm interior.
- Cleanup on the floor object specifically when respawning underground→sky on water hit. The full despawn-then-respawn cycle handles all 6 objects together.

The defensive `OnPlayerDeployed` hook handles the unlikely re-deploy case.
5. **(NO combat HUD destruction.)** Per user direction: spectator keeps the combat HUD so they can watch tickets, flags, capture progress unfold during the match. The body sits at HQ; the HUD reflects team-level state for their team's perspective. 7. **Cross-viewer broadcast:**
   - `refreshAllVisiblePlayerReadyPanels()` — every viewer's COACH button updates to "slot taken" disabled-grey.
8. **Match auto-start check:**
   - `tryAutoStartMatchIfAllReady(eventPlayer)` — with one fewer active player + spectator marked ready, the gate may now be satisfiable.

**Helper bodies:**

```ts
function attachSpectatorCameraIfDeployed(eventPlayer: mod.Player, pid: number): void {
    if (!isPlayerDeployed(eventPlayer)) return;
    const camId = ACTIVE_MAP_CONFIG?.spectatorCameraId;
    if (camId === undefined) return;
    try {
        mod.SetCameraTypeForPlayer(eventPlayer, (mod.Cameras as any).Fixed, camId);
    } catch (e) {
        try { console.log(`[spectator] camera attach threw for pid=${pid} cam=${camId}: ${String(e)}`); } catch {}
    }
}
```

```ts
try {
    if (State.players.spectatorPid === pid) attachSpectatorCameraIfDeployed(eventPlayer, pid);

```

Same helper, idempotent. The pid-match check ensures it's a no-op for non-spectator deploys.

## Boundary OOB Skip (Mitigation C-specific)

`getDesiredBoundaryViolationKind` in [`src/boundary/enforcement.ts:223`](../src/boundary/enforcement.ts#L223):

```ts
function getDesiredBoundaryViolationKind(player: mod.Player): BoundaryPromptKind | undefined {
    if (!isValidPlayer(player)) return undefined;
    if (!isPlayerDeployed(player)) return undefined;
    if (!isPlayerAliveForBoundary(player)) return undefined;
    if (State.match.isEnded) return undefined;

    const pid = safeGetPlayerId(player);
    if (pid === undefined) return undefined;
+   if (pid === State.players.spectatorPid) return undefined; // Mitigation C: spectator body is parked, ignore zone state
    const state = State.round.boundary.zoneStateByPid[pid];
    if (!state) return undefined;
    ...
}
```

Single line. Adds one branch on a hot path — second-tick boundary classification — but the cost is one numeric equality check + one nullable read; on-par with the existing `pid === undefined` check above it. Honest framing per umbrella plan §"Hot path notes."

**Why this matters even though the spectator is parked at HQ:** during the deploy queue + camera-attach window, the engine may briefly consider their body in some other zone state. Without the skip, a "prelive_main_base" or "ground_combat_zone" warning could flash on their Fixed camera view (even though they can't act on it). The skip kills the entire OOB UI surface for them.

## Out of Scope (Ship 2)

(Carries forward from umbrella plan unchanged.)

- **Mid-match spectator exit.** One-way commitment per user direction. Re-entry to play requires disconnect + reconnect or match-end.
- **Spectator-controlled camera.** Static Godot camera per map; no path / free-fly / preset modes.
- **Multiple simultaneous spectators.** Single-slot only.
- **Spectator team scoreboard hide.** Spectator's row shows on the scoreboard with K/D=0/0 under their original team. Cosmetic; no hide unless requested.
- **Triple-tap-during-spectate hardening.** If a spectator triple-taps and re-opens the Player Ready Panel, they could click SWAP (re-team) or interact with other buttons. SWAP especially could put a spectator on the other team while keeping the spectator slot claimed — incoherent state. Defensive hardening (e.g., gate triple-tap on `pid !== State.players.spectatorPid`, OR disable SWAP/READY when spectating) is OUT OF SCOPE for Ship 2. Flagged for follow-up if MP testing reveals it as a real issue.
- **Capture / engagement classifier skip for spectator pid.** Spectator's body sits at HQ which on all current maps is geometrically separate from capture flag triggers. If MP testing reveals trigger overlap and the spectator showing up in engagement counts, add a parallel skip to the boundary one (out-of-scope until empirically observed).
- **Body re-deploy management.** If the spectator's body somehow re-deploys mid-spectate (suicide-while-spectating not blocked, vehicle-spawn collision, etc.), the defensive `OnPlayerDeployed` hook re-attaches the camera but does not actively prevent the re-deploy. Active prevention (blocking the engine deploy queue for spectator pid) is OUT OF SCOPE.
