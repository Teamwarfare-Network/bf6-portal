# TWL Conquest — Known Issues (v1.395)
 - **1: When you first connect or team switch there is a brief period where you cannot do anything, just wait momentarily**
   - During this time you'll see some minor flickering of UI elements.
 - **2: Possible inability to spawn on flags (B) from the deploy screen.** 
   - Screenshot and report this if you see it.
 - **3: Known issues for everyone when a player re-joins mid-round:**
   - If you redeploy at the same moment your respawn time may be longer than usual and this can also impact your Air Deploy or Ground Deploy success (so if it fails, just try again).
   - There is a minor performance hitch (time nudge) spike when they re-join (this will be most noticeable pre-game before it's live).
